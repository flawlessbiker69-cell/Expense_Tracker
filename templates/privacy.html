Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, store your expenses, and show you summaries and spending breakdowns. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database associated with your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
